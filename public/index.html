About

Why this tool exists.

Today's performance marketing demands massive content volume at breakneck speed, creating a severe bandwidth crunch. Brands naturally turn to AI, but standard models just rephrase text to sound "poetic". Because teams can't manually A/B test thousands of daily variations, copy is often approved on a subjective "hunch" — prioritizing what sounds nice over what actually influences a purchase.

I realized the industry desperately needs objective certainty before going live. That's why I built Right Copywriter.

You can simply input your requirements, and the tool automatically generates persuasive copy designed to influence purchase. Already have a draft? Run it through Bias Audit to evaluate and rephrase it for maximum conversions. By quantifying success pre-launch, we shift your marketing from a hopeful "might work" to a scientific "will work."

This isn't a generic AI hallucinating internet pointers. I trained this custom engine using my 6 years in Consumer Insights — feeding it my handwritten ethnographic notes on exactly why real people buy and why they walk away. It also captures more than 60 human biases that influence purchase along with the gist of some of the best marketing research ever done.

Because at the end of the day, marketing isn't about sounding cool. If it doesn't sell, it isn't the right copy.

How Does This Work

You describe your brand, your message, and what you want the copy to do. The engine takes it from there — selecting the most relevant psychological principles for your specific brief, your format, and your audience, then writing copy that is engineered to those principles rather than written by feel.

Every output tells you exactly which principle was used and why — so you are not just getting copy, you are getting a rationale you can defend.

The Behavioral Score

Every piece of copy is evaluated against a proprietary framework built from decades of consumer psychology research. The score reflects how many of the relevant psychological levers are being activated — and how many are being left on the table.

It is not a readability score. It is not a sentiment score. It is a measure of how hard your copy is working at a subconscious level — the level where purchase decisions are actually made.

The Bias Audit shows you the gap between your current copy and its potential — and then closes it.
